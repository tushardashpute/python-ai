# Week 6: Frameworks, Multi-Agent Patterns & Production

## 🎯 Week Overview

Week 6 synthesizes everything: frameworks, multi-agent patterns, and deployment considerations. We'll look at higher-level frameworks that help assemble production AI systems.

### What You'll Master This Week:
1. **Day 1**: Frameworks and integrations (MCP, LangChain-style patterns)
2. **Day 2**: Multi-agent coordination and deployment patterns

---

## 📚 Daily Breakdown

### **Day 1: Frameworks & Integrations**
**Duration:** 2 hours | **Level:** Advanced

**Topics Covered:**
- Popular frameworks and when to use them
- Integrating tools, retrieval, and function-calling in frameworks
- Best practices for modular, testable components

**Intro (for beginners):**
Frameworks provide pre-built patterns and components (like retrieval, tool integration, and orchestration) so you can assemble systems faster and with fewer errors.

---

### **Day 2: Multi-Agent Patterns & Production**
**Duration:** 2 hours | **Level:** Advanced

**Topics Covered:**
- Multi-agent coordination and communication
- Autonomy boundaries and safety in multi-agent systems
- Deployment concerns: scaling, observability, and cost management

**Intro (for beginners):**
Multi-agent systems split work into specialized agents (retrievers, summarizers, action agents). Think of them as a small team where each agent has a clear role and communicates results.

---

## 🚀 Week 6 Practice Projects

1. Build a small multi-agent workflow that coordinates retrieval + summarization
2. Integrate a framework to manage retries, logging and metrics
3. Prepare a minimal deployment checklist (monitoring, costs, fallbacks)
